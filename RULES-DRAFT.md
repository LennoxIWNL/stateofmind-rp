# Server rules (draft for the owner)

A draft in enforceable terms. The owner must review, change anything, and approve before it is published. It reflects mechanics that exist in the code. Penalties are left to staff policy.

## 1. Roleplay
- **Stay in character.** What you say and do is what your character would. OOC talk goes in `(( ))` or the OOC channel and is kept short.
- **Fear for your life (FRP).** When someone points a weapon at you, or you are outnumbered and outgunned, you act as your character would value their life. Running into gunfire for no reason is a breach.
- **Value your character's injuries.** After being downed you remember being hurt and you do not return to the same fight.
- **New life.** When you are killed you forget the events leading to your death, and you do not return to the area for 15 minutes unless staff say otherwise. If you were knocked out and revived, you remember.

## 2. Defined breaches
- **Metagaming:** acting on information your character has not learned in game. This includes anything from streams, Discord, another character, or an out of game conversation. Staff may ask for the source of how your character knew.
- **Powergaming:** forcing an outcome on another player without letting them respond, or using game mechanics to force an action (for example blocking someone in so they cannot play). Emotes must give the other person room to react.
- **Griefing:** actions whose aim is to ruin another player's time with no roleplay reason, such as repeated ramming, killing on sight with no interaction, or camping a spawn.
- **Combat logging:** disconnecting to avoid an arrest, a robbery or a scene. A scene leaves your character where it happened. Jail time and bail are server side and are not removed by reconnecting.
- **Exploiting:** using a bug, a duplicated item (a copied dongle or marked bill does nothing), or an out of map position for gain. Report it with `/report` instead; the first honest report is not punished.
- **Cheating:** any third party tool, script injection or modified client. Permanent ban.
- **Real money trading:** selling items, vehicles, characters or in game money for real money.

## 3. Crime
- **No wanted level.** Police find out through calls, witnesses, cameras and investigation. Do not announce your crimes in OOC.
- **Heists** need a leader, a dongle, a crew within the stated size and a minimum number of police on duty. Hostage taking must be roleplayed and cannot be done as a shortcut to avoid a gunfight.
- **Gangs** hold turf by tagging, contesting and tagging again. Selling drugs outside your own turf is not a feature; do not try to work around the ownership check.
- **Robbery targets** must respect safe zones (hospital, police station interior, spawn) set by staff.

## 4. Police and services
- Police follow the court and jail process: no searching without cause, no jailing without a case, bail and warrants go through a judge.
- Medics treat anyone regardless of faction. Do not use medical or police roles for personal gain.

## 5. Communication
- Keep voice in character. No soundboards, no music through the voice channel.
- Phone social media posts are in character and stay within the post rules. Impersonating police or government handles is a breach.

## 6. Staff
- Use `/report` for help. Staff may teleport to you, bring you or spectate you; every use is logged.
- Staff decisions can be appealed in the Discord within 7 days. Staff do not play favourites and do not use their powers for their own character.

## Owner decisions needed
- Penalty ladder (warning, kick, temporary ban, permanent ban) and who may issue each.
- The new life timer and radius, the safe zones list, and the appeal route.
- Whether the rules are shown in game on first connect, in the loading screen, or only on Discord.
